# Story guide

**Nothing religious or political anywhere in the app** (owner rule, brief #1): no religion, faith, churches or religious holidays, no politics, parties, elections, protests or political figures, in lessons, examples, scenes, art or the exam. Keep life in Berlin everyday: cafés, work, family, sport, transport, food, weather.

One cast, one Berlin, one slow story that runs under the whole course. Content writes lessons from this file; story keeps it. Every lesson adds facts to the continuity log (bottom) when it merges. If a lesson needs a fact that is not here, it adds it to its PR and story approves it in the review.

Rules that keep it from reading like a textbook (also `docs/specs/visual-direction.md`, "Don't look AI-made"):

- Every scene has a reason to care: someone wants something, something is late, wrong or surprising. Never "meet and chat".
- Characters sound like themselves. Herr Weber is dry and exact, Ana is quick and self-correcting, Lena is blunt and distracted, Murat is calm and a little theatrical about coffee.
- German people say: *Kein Problem, Bis gleich, Na ja, Klar, Genau, Stimmt, Moment*. No exclamation marks for effect, no "Great job!", no emoji.
- Callbacks beat repetition: a new lesson uses one person, place or object from an earlier lesson in a new way.
- The learner (`{name}`, from `{city}`) is a person in the room, not a camera. Others react to what they say.

## Places

- **Volkshochschule am Hermannplatz, room 2.04.** Second floor, window onto the U8 line, a board, a map of Europe (from lesson 4), 7 chairs. The radiator knocks. Course days: **Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00.** 
- **The café next to the school** (name on the awning appears in lesson 15: *Café Eck*). Break at 10:30. Counter on the left, one table by the window (Lena's), a chalkboard menu, the stamp card by the till. Murat's espresso machine hisses when someone is about to ask for something.

## Weekly rhythm

Course days are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00, room 2.04. The break at 10:30 is at the café. A lesson is set in the classroom or, when the topic is food, money or small talk, in the café, and the day is always named in the scene. Weekends are outside the course: lessons that need one (hobbies, invitations) start on Thursday with a plan for Saturday and the next Tuesday says what happened.

## Running threads (small mysteries, with where they pay off)

1. **Weber's phone call (set up lesson 1).** The phone rings in the first minute and he steps out. Reveal in lessons 23–24 (family): it was his daughter **Clara**, who studies in Lisbon and calls on Tuesdays at 9:00 because it is the only time she is awake before her lab. He keeps the call short so he is on time. Callbacks: the phone rings again at the start of other Tuesdays (lesson 7: *Gute Frage. Nächste Frage.*; the class guesses wife or girlfriend), once he says *Moment, bitte* and the class says it for him.
2. **Lena's phone and clock (lesson 4, then 6–8, 9).** Lena is waiting for an answer from a venue about a Saturday slot. In lesson 8 the venue says maybe (*Vielleicht ist nicht nein*). We never say what until lesson 39. Small signs: she checks the time, turns her phone face down when Weber looks, and says *Ach so* when someone mentions Friday. Payoff in lessons 39–40 (hobbies, music) and 45–46 (invitation): the bar says yes, she invites the whole course.
3. **Ana's reason (lesson 3 onwards).** Ana joined late in a hurry and avoids saying why she came to Berlin. First dodge in lesson 5: asked where she works, she says the break is over, then *Später*. Lesson 7: *nächste Frage, bitte*. Lesson 8: she stays behind to ask Weber about the B1 exam and answers *Frage? Welche Frage?* Reveal in lessons 25–26 (jobs): she is an architect in Mexico City; in Germany her degree needs recognition, which needs B1. Until then she works at a bakery. Callback: she measures things with her hands and counts rooms.
4. **Murat's stamp card (lesson 5 onwards, "he knows your order").** From lesson 15 the card has stamps. At lesson 16 (paying) it has nine; the tenth coffee is free. The Done stamp in the app can echo it.
5. **The seventh chair (lesson 9).** Ana counts the room twice and gets *sechs*, then *sieben* when Tomasz arrives. Tomasz is late every Thursday after that, and always has an excuse in a number (*Die U8 ist spät*, lesson 19). Never explained; it is a running joke.
6. **The map of Europe (lesson 4).** Weber hung it beside the board. Pins appear for every country mentioned in the course. By lesson 60 it is full.

## Chapter arcs (each ends in a review stop, the last in the A1 exam)

1. **Lessons 1–12, "Der Kurs".** A group of strangers become a class. Ends with Weber's first test and the café after it.
2. **13–24, "Das Café und zu Hause".** Food, ordering, the learner's flat, time, family. Ends with Weber's call and Clara.
3. **25–36, "Die Stadt".** Jobs, transport, directions, shopping, the doctor (Lena's hand, hurt at football). Ends with Ana's job.
4. **37–48, "Freizeit".** Days, hobbies, weather, invitations, travel. Ends with Lena's gig.
5. **49–60, "Die Reise".** Booking, hotel, forms, dates, colours, places, repair phrases. Ends on the day before the A1 exam; Weber hands each student a task card.

## Engagement ideas (for the owner)

1. **A last line that pulls you forward.** The Done screen ends with one short German line from the last scene that points to the next one (*Bis Donnerstag. Lena hat eine Überraschung.*). Needs a `next` field in the lesson and a line on the Done screen (ux).
2. **Callbacks in the review.** Warm-up and review cards use a sentence from an earlier scene with the speaker's face (Lena: *Ich trinke drei Kaffee.* → how many?). No new UI, only the cast bust next to a card.
3. **The map of Europe fills up** as countries are learned, shown on the course map as a small pin count. Needs ux.
4. **Murat's stamp card** as the progress stamp on Done: nine stamps and a free coffee at the end of a chapter.
5. **Characters in the exam**: the A1 exam tasks use these people and these places, so the exam feels like the last scene of the course.

None of these changes anything until the owner says yes; 1 and 2 are content-only.
