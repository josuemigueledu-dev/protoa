# Lesson plan 15–19

For the owner's sign-off. Once approved, content builds lessons 15 to 19 from this file, one per run, in the lesson format (`content.js` header) and under `docs/specs/lesson-experience.md`: items are labelled word or phrase, learners only pick or build words already taught, no typing in lessons 1–3, one typed word from lesson 4, whole short sentences from lesson 11. Owner rule holds everywhere: nothing religious or political. Every headword below was checked against lessons 1–9 and plan 10–14, so none repeats; each lesson has 10–14 items (phrases counted). Lessons 15–19 are in chapter 2, "Das Café und zu Hause". Lesson 16 is the **review stop** (after 4, 8, 12, 16): its scene samples words from lessons 13–16.

## Lesson 15: Ordering

- **Station:** Karl-Marx-Straße. **Grammar:** *Ich möchte* + accusative (*einen* for masculine, *eine*, *ein*), and the polite *Ich hätte gern …* as a fixed phrase. *möchten*: ich möchte, du möchtest, er möchte, wir möchten, Sie möchten. **ES contrast:** *quisiera* = *möchte*: both are the polite "I would like". *Ich will* is as blunt as *quiero* said to a waiter; use *möchte*.
- **Words (13):** möchten, bestellen, die Karte, die Suppe, der Salat, das Brötchen, das Mittagessen, vegetarisch (words); *Ich hätte gern*, *Was darf es sein?*, *Sonst noch etwas?*, *zum Mitnehmen*, *Bitte schön* (phrases, with parts shown).
- **Warm-up (lessons 6, 13, 14):** der Kaffee, die Milch, essen, lecker.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 12:30, the café, after class. Ana is at work (the bakery, thread 3: only "Ana hat Arbeit"), Lena has a rehearsal. The learner comes alone for the first time at lunch, the queue is six people long, and Murat says nothing: for weeks he has known the usual order, today he waits to be asked. Choose from the chalkboard card: *Suppe, Salat, Brötchen*. The learner has to order a soup, say *zum Mitnehmen*, and answer *Sonst noch etwas?*
- **Who:** the learner and the barista. Murat's name is not said until the end.
- **Twist:** Murat turns his name tag round: **Murat**. He takes a stamp card out of a drawer. It has nine stamps: he has been stamping it for weeks without telling anyone. *Noch einen Kaffee, dann ist einer frei.* **New fact:** the learner's card has nine stamps after today (thread 4; the outline's "nine at lesson 16" starts here). Nobody else in the café knew the card existed.
- **Change from the outline:** none. Name tag and stamp card are in this lesson as planned; Murat's line uses *Kaffee*, a word the learner knows.

## Lesson 16: Paying (review stop)

- **Station:** Karl-Marx-Straße (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** *Was kostet …? / Das kostet …*; prices read "zwei Euro fünfzig" (no word for cents, *Euro* does not change); tens: 30 to 100, and 21 to 99 with the units first (*einundzwanzig*), but the learner meets only a few of those. **ES contrast:** Spanish *treinta y uno* puts the tens first; German says "one-and-thirty" (units first). *ein Euro*, not *eins Euro*.
- **Words (13):** dreißig, vierzig, fünfzig, sechzig, siebzig, achtzig, neunzig, hundert, der Euro, die Rechnung, bezahlen, kosten, *Stimmt so*.
- **Warm-up (lessons 10, 13, 15):** zwanzig, die Tasse, möchten, bestellen.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 10:30, the café, the break. The learner's tenth coffee is free (the stamp card). Lena has forgotten her wallet and a call is due in twenty minutes (thread 2). The bill is wrong by one euro: Murat reads it out, and the learner has to hear that it says *neun Euro fünfzig* when the board says *acht Euro fünfzig*.
- **Who:** the learner, Lena, Ana (on her way to the bakery), Murat.
- **Twist:** the learner pays for Lena without being asked and says *Kein Problem* (callback to lesson 1). Lena says *Danke*, then is quiet. **New fact:** Lena never says thank you twice; this is the first *Danke* she means. Murat stamps the card and hands over the free coffee without a word.
- **Review stop:** after the scene, the review sampling uses words from lessons 13 to 16.
- **Change from the outline:** the free coffee is the tenth, so the card shows nine going in (set up in 15), and the number practice is the bill, not an exercise list.

## Lesson 17: Rooms

- **Station:** Schönhauser Allee. **Grammar:** *Das ist die Küche. / Hier ist das Bad.* Predicate adjectives (*groß*, *klein*) never change after *ist*. Use the gender from lesson 13 (every noun with its article). **ES contrast:** *cuarto* covers both *Zimmer* (room) and *Schlafzimmer* (bedroom); German has a word for each kind of room, so learn each by name.
- **Words (13):** die Wohnung, das Zimmer, die Küche, das Bad, das Wohnzimmer, das Schlafzimmer, der Flur, der Balkon, die Tür, das Fenster, groß, klein, bleiben.
- **Warm-up (lessons 14, 16):** die Zwiebel, die Tomate, hundert, die Rechnung.
- **Scene:** Saturday, 19:00, Ana's flat in Neukölln. The dinner Ana promised in lesson 14 is cooking: the same tomatoes and onions from the market are in a pot on the stove, and the kitchen is the biggest room. Ana shows the learner the flat in a straight line: three rooms off one long hallway, and a balcony the size of a table. The learner answers each room with *groß* or *klein*.
- **Who:** Ana, the learner, Lena (arrives at the end).
- **Twist:** the doorbell. Lena arrives with her guitar case, a bag and a pillow. *Ich bleibe heute hier. Okay?* Ana: *Natürlich.* She is already unrolling a mattress. **New fact:** Lena stays at Ana's tonight (her own flat is not available until Monday; nobody asks why). She stays a second night in lesson 21.
- **Change from the outline:** the outline had "she is staying the night" as the twist; *bleiben* makes it Lena's own line.

## Lesson 18: Furniture, *es gibt*

- **Station:** Schönhauser Allee (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** *Es gibt + accusative*: *Es gibt einen Tisch / eine Lampe / ein Sofa*; *keinen / keine / kein* for "there is no". *haben*: ich habe, du hast, er hat, wir haben. **ES contrast:** *hay* = *es gibt* (one form for singular and plural, like *hay*); *tener* = *haben*, but *haben* does not express age (lesson 10: *ich bin … Jahre alt*).
- **Words (13):** haben, es gibt, der Stuhl, der Tisch, das Bett, das Sofa, der Schrank, das Regal, die Lampe, der Teppich, kein, da, *Moment mal* (phrase).
- **Warm-up (lessons 9, 17):** sieben, die Tür, das Fenster, das Zimmer.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 18:00, the learner's flat in Neukölln: the first time the course comes to the learner. Ana and Lena help move the table to the window and ask what there is: *Es gibt einen Tisch. Es gibt kein Sofa. Es gibt keinen Stuhl.* The learner is hosting the class on Saturday morning, and has a table for seven but only one chair.
- **Who:** the learner, Ana, Lena, and Tomasz at the door.
- **Twist:** Tomasz is late again and carries a folding chair from the bike shop (callback to the seventh chair, thread 5). No excuse yet. Lena counts: *Wir haben einen Stuhl, zwei Stühle …* and runs out of fingers. Ana: *Noch nicht genug.* **New fact:** Tomasz brings a chair "from the shop" every time he is late; this is the first.
- **Change from the outline:** the story guide had the flat's "seventh chair" joke in lesson 9; here it pays off with a physical chair.

## Lesson 19: The clock

- **Station:** Alexanderplatz. **Grammar:** *Wie spät ist es? Es ist drei Uhr.*; *halb drei* = 2:30 (half *to* three); *Viertel nach / vor*; the 24-hour form for trains (*vierzehn Uhr*). **ES contrast:** *y cuarto / menos cuarto* are *Viertel nach / vor*, but *halb drei* is 2:30 (Spanish *las tres y media* is 3:30). This is the trap of the lesson.
- **Words (13):** die Uhr, die Stunde, die Minute, halb, das Viertel, vor, nach, früh, spät, pünktlich, die U-Bahn, der Zug, *Wie spät ist es?* (phrase).
- **Warm-up (lessons 10, 18):** vierzehn, die Nummer, das Bett, haben.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 8:45, the U8 platform at Hermannplatz. Weber has moved the lesson outside: *Heute lernen wir die Uhr auf dem Bahnsteig.* He asks the class to read the display (*Wittenau 8:57*, *Wittenau 9:03*) and say how long the wait is. The train is late; the clock on the platform and Weber's wristwatch disagree by three minutes and he wants an explanation.
- **Who:** Weber, Ana, Lena, the learner.
- **Twist:** Tomasz steps off the late train, out of breath: *Die U8 ist spät.* Weber: *Das sehe ich.* Then Lena, looking at her phone, to nobody: *Vierzehn Uhr.* **New fact:** Lena has a call at 14:00 today (thread 2) and it matters enough that she checks the time twice. Weber notices and says nothing.
- **Change from the outline:** the outline's "train times come in" is the board reading; Tomasz's number excuse (thread 5, *Die U8 ist spät*) is placed here and not in lesson 18.
