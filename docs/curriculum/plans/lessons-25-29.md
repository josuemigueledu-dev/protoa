# Lesson plan 25–29

For the owner's sign-off. Once approved, content builds lessons 25 to 29 from this file, one per run, in the lesson format (`content.js` header) and under `docs/specs/lesson-experience.md`: items are labelled word or phrase, learners only pick or build words already taught, whole short sentences are typed from lesson 11 on with the word bank one tap away. Facts come from `docs/curriculum/story.md`; anything this plan adds is marked **new fact** and goes into the continuity log when the lesson merges.

Owner rule holds everywhere: nothing religious or political. Every headword below was checked against lessons 1–13 and plans 14–24, so none repeats; each lesson has 12–13 items (phrases counted).

Lessons 25–29 open chapter 3, **"Unterwegs"** (the chapter banner in the app, `CHAPTERS` in content.js; comes before lesson 25). The chapter moves the course out of room 2.04 and into the city. Its first two lessons pay off thread 3 (Ana's reason), as `story.md` fixes: she is an architect, her degree needs B1 to be recognised, and until then she works at a bakery. Lessons 27–29 are one trip, Tuesday's plan and Thursday's journey to Zoologischer Garten and Wittenbergplatz. Lesson 28 is the **review stop** (after 4, 8, … 28): its scene samples words from lessons 25–28.

## Lesson 25: Jobs

- **Station:** Eberswalder Straße. **Grammar:** a job takes no article: *Ich bin Lehrer. Lena ist Kellnerin.* A woman's job adds *-in* (*der Lehrer / die Lehrerin*, as *Student / Studentin* in lesson 3), sometimes with an umlaut (*der Arzt / die Ärztin*, *der Koch / die Köchin*). *Was sind Sie von Beruf?* is the question. **ES contrast:** this is one place where Spanish helps and English misleads: *soy arquitecta* has no article and neither does *Ich bin Architektin*; "I am **an** architect" makes learners say *eine Architektin*. German *-in* does the job of Spanish *-a*.
- **Words (12):** der Beruf, die Arbeit, der Job, der Chef, die Architektin, die Bäckerei, der Arzt, der Koch, der Verkäufer, der Kellner, eigentlich (words); *Was sind Sie von Beruf?* (phrase, parts shown).
- **Warm-up (lessons 2, 3, 5, 6):** der Lehrer, die Studentin, arbeiten, kochen.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 9:00, room 2.04, the first lesson of the new chapter. Weber writes two words on the board, *Arbeit* and *Beruf*, and goes round the room: *Was sind Sie von Beruf?* Tomasz: *Verkäufer*, in the bike shop (lesson 10). Yuki: *Köchin*, in a restaurant in Mitte. Lena: *Kellnerin*, in a café in Kreuzberg, and *am Freitag Musikerin*. The learner answers with `{field}` (default *Informatik*) and Weber writes it on the board without comment. Every answer is one exercise: the learner builds *Lena ist Kellnerin*, never *eine Kellnerin*.
- **Who:** Weber, Ana, Lena, Tomasz, Yuki, the learner.
- **Twist:** Ana is last. *Ich arbeite in der Bäckerei.* Weber, who heard her question about the B1 exam in lesson 8, asks only: *Das ist Ihre Arbeit. Und Ihr Beruf?* The room is quiet. Ana: *Sie wissen das, Herr Weber.* Then, to the class: *Eigentlich bin ich Architektin.* Lena: *Ach so. Die Hände.* (Ana measures everything with her hands, lessons 14 and 17.) Ana asks the learner, not Weber: *Ist das komisch, {name}? Eine Architektin in der Bäckerei?* The learner's typed answer is the line the lesson builds towards: *Die Bäckerei ist dein Job. Architektin ist dein Beruf.* **New facts:** Ana is an architect from Mexico City and works at a bakery in Neukölln for now (thread 3, first half of the reveal); Lena works as a waitress in a café in Kreuzberg; Yuki is a cook.
- **Change from the outline:** none; this is the first beat of chapter 3.

## Lesson 26: Study and office

- **Station:** Eberswalder Straße (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** *als* + job, no article: *Ich arbeite als Architektin.* *studieren* for a subject at university (*Ich studiere Informatik*), *lernen* for learning in general (lesson 5). Formal *Sie* at an office desk: *Haben Sie einen Termin?* **ES contrast:** *trabajo como arquitecta* is *Ich arbeite als Architektin*: here *como* is *als*, not *wie*, and *Ich arbeite wie Architektin* means "I work like an architect". Words in *-tät* are *die* and match Spanish *-dad*: *die Universität* / *la universidad*.
- **Words (13):** das Büro, die Firma, der Termin, der Computer, der Kollege, studieren, das Studium, die Universität, die Prüfung, als, wichtig, fertig (words); *Auf Deutsch, bitte* (phrase, parts shown).
- **Warm-up (lessons 19, 22, 25):** pünktlich, der Monat, der Beruf, die Arbeit.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 13:00, after class. Ana has a *Termin* at an architects' office on Kastanienallee, a short walk from Eberswalder Straße: her first in Germany. She asked the learner to come because four ears hear more than two. Reception, a plant, five computers, everyone in black. The receptionist asks for the name and the appointment; the learner helps Ana with the forms of address (*Frau Brandt*, *Sie*). Frau Brandt, *die Chefin* (lesson 25), asks about the *Studium*: Ana studied at the *Universität* in Mexico City, five years, *fertig*, and then worked six years *als Architektin* (content words it without *seit*, which comes later). Frau Brandt is friendly and exact: the plans are good, but for the job German is *wichtig*: *Sie brauchen B1.*
- **Who:** Ana, the learner, Frau Brandt (new, head of the office, 50s), the receptionist (one line).
- **Twist:** Frau Brandt sees Ana struggling and switches to Spanish: she studied in Barcelona. *¿Prefiere en español?* Ana, who slips into Spanish when she is tired (lesson 13, *sin … ohne*), says: *Nein. Auf Deutsch, bitte.* Frau Brandt switches back and smiles for the first time: *Gut. Wann ist die Prüfung?* Ana: *Im Juni.* *Dann kommen Sie im Juni wieder. Mit B1.* On the way out Ana says to the learner, in German: *Im Juni. Das ist nicht viel Zeit.* **New facts:** Ana's B1 exam is in June; the office of Frau Brandt will see her again with B1 (thread 3 revealed: the reason she is in the course; the payoff at the end of chapter 3, lesson 36, is a call from this office). The learner now knows why Ana is in a hurry.
- **Change from the outline:** the outline left lesson 26 open; the office visit turns the reveal of lesson 25 into a goal with a date, so the rest of A1 has something at stake.

## Lesson 27: Vehicles

- **Station:** Zoologischer Garten. **Grammar:** two verbs whose vowel changes for *du* and *er/sie*: *fahren* (*du fährst, er fährt*) and *nehmen* (*du nimmst, er nimmt*). *mit dem Bus, mit der U-Bahn, mit dem Fahrrad* are learned as whole phrases (the case behind *dem/der* is A2). **ES contrast:** Spanish *ir* covers walking and riding; German does not: *gehen* is on foot (lesson 7), *fahren* is on wheels. *Ich gehe mit dem Bus* is wrong; *Ich fahre mit dem Bus*, or *Ich gehe zu Fuß*. *ir en metro* is *mit der U-Bahn fahren*: *en* becomes *mit*.
- **Words (12):** fahren, nehmen, der Bus, die S-Bahn, die Straßenbahn, das Fahrrad, das Auto, das Taxi, weit, dauern, lange (words); *zu Fuß* (phrase, parts shown). Note on the card: Berliners mostly say *die Tram*.
- **Warm-up (lessons 7, 19):** gehen, die U-Bahn, der Zug, die Minute.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 9:00, room 2.04. Weber hangs the BVG network map beside the map of Europe. On Thursday the course meets outside: *Donnerstag, 9:00, Bahnhof Zoo.* Everyone says how they will get there and how long it takes: *Wie lange dauert das?* The learner and Ana live in Neukölln: *Wir nehmen die U7 und dann die U9. Vierzig Minuten.* Yuki takes the S-Bahn. Lena says *Taxi* and the class says *Lena!*; she changes it to the bike. Weber does not have a car: *Ich gehe zu Fuß.* From Wedding? *Eine Stunde. Das ist nicht weit.*
- **Who:** Weber, Ana, Lena, Tomasz, Yuki, the learner.
- **Twist:** Weber writes *9:00* on the board, underlines it twice and looks at Tomasz. Tomasz: *Mit dem Fahrrad bin ich pünktlich.* Ana: *Ein Kaffee, Tomasz: du bist nicht pünktlich.* Tomasz: *Ein Kaffee: ich bin pünktlich.* They shake on it, and Weber says *Das sehen wir.* **New facts:** Weber has no car and walks everywhere; Ana and Tomasz have a bet of one coffee at Murat's (payoff lesson 28).
- **Change from the outline:** none.

## Lesson 28: Tickets and stops (review stop)

- **Station:** Zoologischer Garten (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** a route in two pieces: *mit der U7 bis Berliner Straße, dann die U9 Richtung Osloer Straße.* *bis* (lesson 20) is the stop where you get off, *Richtung* + the last stop names the direction. *nächste* in fixed phrases: *die nächste Station*, *der nächste Zug*. **ES contrast:** *dirección Osloer Straße* is *Richtung Osloer Straße*, with no *a* and no article; *hasta* is *bis*. *die Karte* (the menu, lesson 15) is also the bank card here: same word, so the learner hears it in context.
- **Words (12):** die Fahrkarte, der Automat, der Bahnhof, die Haltestelle, die Linie, die Richtung, nächste, die Kontrolle, stempeln, gültig, kaputt (words); *Die Fahrkarten, bitte* (phrase, parts shown).
- **Warm-up (lessons 16, 25–27):** bezahlen, kosten, der Beruf, fahren, das Fahrrad.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 8:20, the U7 platform at Rathaus Neukölln. The learner and Ana go to Zoo together. The first ticket machine is *kaputt*; at the second, Ana's Mexican bank card does not work, so the learner pays for both (as for Lena in lesson 16; the price is the real BVG single AB fare, read off the screen as numbers from lesson 16). Ana walks to the train with her ticket in her hand and the learner stops her: *Ana, stempeln!* Ana: *Stempeln? Wie bei Murat?* (thread 4, the stamp card). On the U7 they talk about Frau Brandt (lesson 26) and the learner's own work (lesson 25): the review questions. At Berliner Straße they change to the U9 *Richtung Osloer Straße*, three stops. Two people in ordinary jackets stand up: *Die Fahrkarten, bitte.* Both tickets are stamped and *gültig*. The man by the door has none. Ana, quietly: *Sechzig Euro?* The learner: *Sechzig Euro.* Ana: *Danke, {name}. Wirklich.*
- **Who:** Ana, the learner, two ticket inspectors (one line), then Tomasz and Weber at Zoo.
- **Twist:** at Bahnhof Zoo, 8:55, Tomasz is already there, leaning on his bike. 9:00 sharp, Weber arrives on foot, looks at Tomasz, then at his watch: *Pünktlich.* Tomasz: *Mit dem Fahrrad bin ich immer pünktlich.* Weber: *Dann immer mit dem Fahrrad, bitte.* Ana owes him the coffee (lesson 27) and pays it at Murat's next Tuesday. **New facts:** Tomasz is on time when he comes by bike (thread 5: the excuse is always the train); the learner and Ana travel to the city together.
- **Review stop:** the seventh (after 4, 8, … 28). The questions on the U7 sample words from lessons 25–28 (Ana's *Beruf*, Frau Brandt's *Büro* and *Prüfung*, how long the ride *dauert*, which *Linie*).
- **Change from the outline:** none. The inspection ends well on purpose: the learner did the right thing in time, and the fine happens to someone else.

## Lesson 29: Asking the way

- **Station:** Wittenbergplatz. **Grammar:** *Wo ist …?* and *Wie komme ich zu …?* (*zum Platz, zur Ecke* learned as phrases), and the answer as a polite instruction with the verb first: *Gehen Sie geradeaus. Gehen Sie links.* (the full imperative is lesson 45). **ES contrast:** *a la izquierda* is just *links*, no article. The trap is the right-hand side: *rechts* sounds like Spanish *recto* (straight on), but it means right; straight on is *geradeaus*.
- **Words (13):** links, rechts, geradeaus, die Straße, die Ecke, die Ampel, der Platz, der Stadtplan, zurück, suchen, finden (words); *Wie komme ich zu …?*, *in der Nähe* (phrases, parts shown).
- **Warm-up (lessons 5, 7, 28):** wo, wohin, dort, die Richtung.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 9:20, Wittenbergplatz, one stop from Zoo. Weber pairs the class up and gives each pair a card: *Ein Café am Viktoria-Luise-Platz. 10:30.* No phones: he collects them in his hat. Lena looks at hers for a long second before she puts it in, face down. The learner and Ana are a pair; they have a paper *Stadtplan* and the rule that they must ask people. A woman with a dog gives directions fast: *Geradeaus, an der Ampel rechts, dann die zweite links.* The learner asks for it again, slowly (*Langsam, bitte*, lesson 12) and repeats it back. At the lights Ana goes straight on: she heard *rechts* as *recto*. The learner calls her back: *Nein, Ana, rechts! Nicht geradeaus.* Then Ana, the architect, finds the corner first, by the buildings and not by the map.
- **Who:** Weber, Ana, the learner, a passer-by (one or two lines), Lena and Tomasz at the end.
- **Twist:** Weber is already at the café, with a coffee: *Zwölf Minuten. Nicht schlecht.* (lesson 12's only praise; lesson 10's *Siebzehn Minuten* for Tomasz). Lena and Tomasz arrive last: they went *geradeaus* the whole way. Weber hands back the phones. Lena's screen shows three missed calls from the number she gave the class in lesson 10, *die Nummer vom Club*. She says *Einen Moment* (lesson 1) and goes outside to call back. **New fact:** the club called three times while Lena had no phone (thread 2; still unsaid, see note). Cliffhanger for lesson 30.
- **Change from the outline:** none. **Note for lesson 30:** Lena comes back in and says only *Vielleicht. Noch nicht.* (lesson 8, lesson 12). The yes stays in lesson 39.
