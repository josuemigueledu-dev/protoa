# Lesson plan 10–14

For the owner's sign-off. Once approved, content builds lessons 10 to 14 from this file, one per run, in the lesson format (`content.js` header). Facts come from the story guide (`docs/curriculum/story.md`, PR #37); anything this plan adds is marked **new fact** and goes into the guide's continuity log when the lesson merges.

Owner rule holds everywhere: nothing religious or political. Every word below was checked against the words of lessons 1–9, so no lesson repeats a headword. Each lesson has 10–14 words.

Chapter 1 ("Der Kurs") ends at lesson 12 with the review stop and Weber's first test. Lessons 13–14 open chapter 2 ("Das Café und zu Hause").

## Lesson 10: Numbers 11–20, phone numbers, age

- **Station:** Boddinstraße (second lesson of the numbers topic). **Grammar:** *Wie alt …? Ich bin … Jahre alt / X ist … Jahre alt*; phone numbers read in single digits or pairs. **ES contrast:** *tengo 20 años* is *ich bin 20 Jahre alt* (be, not have); *elf, zwölf* are not built like *dreizehn* to *neunzehn* (like *once, doce* vs *trece*).
- **Words (14):** elf, zwölf, dreizehn, vierzehn, fünfzehn, sechzehn, siebzehn, achtzehn, neunzehn, zwanzig, das Jahr, alt, die Nummer, das Handy.
- **Warm-up (from lesson 9):** drei, sieben, zehn, die Zahl.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 9:00, room 2.04. Weber hands round a sheet: a contact list for the course, because he is tired of "Ana, could you ask Lena". Everyone reads their number aloud and the next person writes it down; the room is a chain of digits. Weber starts the age game with objects, not people: *Wie alt ist die Gitarre?* (Lena's guitar: fifteen years old), *Wie alt ist der Pullover?* (Weber's cardigan: twenty).
- **Who:** Weber, Lena, Ana, the learner. Tomasz comes in late again during the list (it is Thursday; the joke starts here, no excuse yet).
- **Twist:** Lena gives her number last, slowly, three times, and makes Ana read it back. She says it is the number "from the club" (she won't say more). Weber writes it on the list without comment and underlines it. **New fact:** Lena has a number for the band that she keeps separate; nobody asks. Thread 2.
- **Age rule for this lesson:** no personal ages above twenty (those come in lesson 16). **New fact:** Tomasz is twenty, from Kraków, and works part time in a bike shop (one line, in the age exercise).
- **Fictional numbers:** use the Bundesnetzagentur fiction range (Berlin 030 23125 xxx) for any full number, never a real-looking mobile number.
- **Change from the outline:** none. The outline's "Lena gives hers last, three times" stands.

## Lesson 11: Joining words

- **Station:** Schönleinstraße. **Grammar:** *und, oder, aber, denn* join two sentences and do not change the verb position (the verb stays second after them). **ES contrast:** *aber* = *pero* but is not *sino*; *denn* = *porque*, never at the start of a question.
- **Words (13):** aber, denn, noch, nur, schon, zusammen, allein, beide, alle, gleich, später, der Tee, der Kuchen.
- **Warm-up (lessons 4–10):** oder, auch, und, das Café.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 10:30, the café. Lena, Ana and the learner share a table. Lena has had three coffees (lesson 9, *drei Kaffee*), and Ana tells her to drink tea. Lena says *Ich trinke Tee, denn ich trinke schon drei Kaffee* (and Ana: *Aber du trinkst Kaffee!*). The argument is about cake: Ana wants one piece for the table, Lena wants her own, the learner is asked to decide: *allein oder zusammen?*
- **Who:** Lena, Ana, the learner, the barista (still unnamed, *Sie*).
- **Twist:** the barista brings two coffees, one tea and a piece of cake, with the line *Kaffee gleich, Kuchen später.* Nobody ordered it that way and it turns out to be exactly what the table wanted.
- **Change from the outline:** the outline's twist, *Kaffee und Kuchen, aber nicht jetzt*, uses *nicht*, which lesson 12 teaches. Murat's line is now *Kaffee gleich, Kuchen später*. Also, only *aber* and *denn* are new connectors; *und, oder, auch* are recalled from lessons 1 and 4.

## Lesson 12: Not and very (review stop)

- **Station:** Schönleinstraße (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** *nicht* goes before the word it negates (*nicht gut*, *nicht schlecht*); *sehr* and *gern*: *Ich lese gern.* **ES contrast:** *gern* has no Spanish word; Spanish says *me gusta leer*. Teach it as a verb add-on that means "with pleasure", not as a verb.
- **Words (13):** nicht, sehr, gern, schlecht, schwer, leicht, schnell, langsam, ein bisschen, zu, der Test, lesen, noch nicht.
- **Warm-up (lessons 9–11):** die Zahl, später, zusammen, aber.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 9:00, room 2.04. Weber's first mini-test: he reads ten short statements and each student says whether it is true, with a short answer. The learner is *nicht sehr gut, aber gern*: slow, careful, one hesitation. Ana says Weber reads *zu schnell*; Weber reads the next one *sehr langsam*, with a straight face.
- **Who:** Weber, Ana, Lena, the learner, and Tomasz (on time for once; Weber notes it).
- **Twist:** Weber returns the tests face down, learner first. The only comment is *Das war nicht schlecht.* The class goes quiet: it is the highest praise he has ever given. **New fact:** Weber's only praise is *nicht schlecht*, and the class knows to hold on to it (payoff: lesson 24).
- **Review stop:** this is the first review stop (after lesson 12); the test scene should therefore sample words from lessons 9–12 in the statements.

## Lesson 13: Drinks

- **Station:** Kottbusser Tor. **Grammar:** nouns have a gender: *der, die, das*, and the matching *ein, eine, ein*; learn every noun with its article. **ES contrast:** *el/la* are two genders, German has three; *das Wasser* is neuter and *der Tee* masculine, so the Spanish word does not predict it (*la leche* is feminine and *die Milch* too, but *el vaso* is masculine and *das Glas* is neuter). Learn each noun with its article.
- **Words (12):** das Wasser, die Milch, der Saft, die Cola, das Bier, der Zucker, das Glas, die Tasse, heiß, kalt, ohne, Prost.
- **Warm-up (lessons 6, 11):** der Kaffee, der Tee, der Kuchen, trinken.
- **Scene:** Thursday, 10:30, the café. Lena orders for the whole table in one breath (*zwei Kaffee, ein Tee, ein Wasser, ohne Zucker, mit Milch*), because she has ten minutes and a call to wait for (thread 2). The barista gets the milk wrong: Ana's coffee comes with milk, and she asked for *ohne*. The learner has to say it again, slowly, with *ohne* and *mit* in the right places.
- **Who:** Lena, Ana, the learner, the barista (still unnamed, *Sie*).
- **Twist:** the barista sets the learner's drink down before the learner has said a word: *Kaffee mit Milch, ohne Zucker.* It is exactly the usual order. **New fact:** the learner's usual order is *Kaffee mit Milch, ohne Zucker*.  Ana notices and raises an eyebrow; Lena says *Ach so* (callback: the learner is a regular).
- **Chapter 2 opens here:** the first lesson set only in the café, no school.

## Lesson 14: Food

- **Station:** Kottbusser Tor (second lesson of the topic). **Grammar:** plural (*die Tomate, die Tomaten*), and the first accusative: *Ich brauche einen Apfel / eine Tomate / ein Ei*; only the masculine changes. **ES contrast:** *cebolla* is *Zwiebel* but both end up in the same pot; *Ei* is neuter.
- **Words (13):** die Tomate, die Zwiebel, das Brot, der Käse, der Apfel, das Ei, der Reis, die Bohne, essen, lecker, frisch, brauchen, das Gemüse.
- **Warm-up (lessons 13, 6):** die Milch, das Wasser, kochen, die Tasse.
- **Scene:** Tuesday, 13:00, the market at Maybachufer, after class. Ana wants to cook for her flat tonight and has a list in Spanish; she and the learner shop together and she needs the German. She measures a tomato with her hand before she buys it. The stall holder answers fast; the learner understands *frisch* and *lecker* and asks for the rest again.
- **Who:** Ana, the learner, a stall holder (new, unnamed, *Sie*), and Lena, who joins in the middle with her guitar case.
- **Twist:** Ana invites the learner and Lena to dinner at her flat, *Samstag, bei mir.* Lena says *Vielleicht* and looks at her phone; Ana: *Das heißt ja.* (callback to lesson 8). **New fact:** dinner at Ana's on a Saturday, payoff in lesson 17 (rooms). **Thread 3 seed:** Ana measures things with her hands and counts without thinking; the stall holder asks if she is a cook and she says *Nein, nein* and changes the subject. Do not say what she does; the reveal is lessons 25–26.
- **Change from the outline:** the market runs on Tuesdays and Fridays, so the scene is Tuesday after class and not Thursday. The invitation is for a Saturday three weeks out in the course's time, which lesson 17 shows.
