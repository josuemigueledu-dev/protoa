# Revenue: five projects, one goal (draft for the owner, Oct 25, 2026)

The owner asked for revenue streams that don't put all eggs in one basket: up to five separate projects aimed at one goal, income, by legitimate means only (no spam, fake reviews, misleading claims or breaking platform rules). Ranges below are rough judgement, not research; they assume the project actually finds customers, which is the hard part. Each extra agent costs plan usage every run.

Common to all of them: the agents build and run the work; **the owner** opens accounts in his name (payments, stores, social), approves anything public, and handles tax and identity checks. Nothing is published or charged without his OK.

## 1. Protoa German, Plus subscription (core)

- **What:** the app we are building, with the free/Plus split from the monetization work (switched off today). Plus: more AI conversation, custom AI lessons, sync.
- **Needs from the owner:** hosting with accounts and sync (platform's launch plan), a Stripe account for the web, Apple and Google developer accounts for the stores, a privacy policy he signs off.
- **First money:** 2–4 months (needs accounts, sync, the store release and the first users).
- **Realistic:** $0–500/month in year one without marketing; more only with a real user base.
- **Team:** the existing five agents.

## 2. Protoa for other languages

- **What:** the same engine and Night Glass look for a second course, e.g. Spanish for English speakers (the biggest market) or German for Spanish speakers. Story, cast and art are new; the code is shared.
- **Needs from the owner:** nothing new beyond project 1; a pick of the language.
- **First money:** after project 1 launches; it multiplies its reach.
- **Realistic:** similar to project 1 per language once live.
- **Team:** one content agent and one story agent per language.

## 3. Digital products from the course

- **What:** sell what we already make, outside the app: a printable "German for Spanish speakers" phrasebook and workbook (PDF plus audio), flashcard decks (Anki), and the painted Berlin scenes as wallpapers or posters.
- **Needs from the owner:** a Gumroad or Etsy account; OK on each product page.
- **First money:** 2–4 weeks.
- **Realistic:** $50–1,000/month, depending on how many products and how well they are found by search.
- **Team:** one agent (writes, lays out, lists, refreshes).

## 4. A small tool for teachers

- **What:** "glossed lesson maker": a teacher pastes a German text and gets a lesson with word-by-word glosses, exercises and audio, using Protoa's lesson engine. Sold to language teachers and schools (Volkshochschulen, tutors) for a small monthly fee.
- **Needs from the owner:** Stripe, a domain, and about 10 teachers to try it free first.
- **First money:** 1–3 months.
- **Realistic:** $0–2,000/month; B2B is slower to start but customers stay.
- **Team:** one agent (build and support), using the AI server we already have.

## 5. A content channel that feeds the others

- **What:** short daily videos and posts: one German phrase in a painted Berlin scene, with Protoa's voice. Brings people to the app and the products; later sponsorship or affiliate income.
- **Needs from the owner:** YouTube/TikTok/Instagram accounts; OK on the format; he posts or approves scheduled posts.
- **First money:** 3–6 months (audience first).
- **Realistic:** $0–500/month directly; its real value is users for projects 1–4.
- **Team:** one agent (scripts, images on the owner's PC, edits).

## Suggested order

Start 3 (fast first money, low effort) and keep 1 on track. Add 4 and 5 once 3 has its first sales. Start 2 after project 1 launches. Total extra cost: one to three more agents.
