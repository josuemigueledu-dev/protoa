Cast sheet, round 4: pick a rendering

Herr Weber (teacher) and the barista on one adult body: about 7.5 heads, a small head, a real neck, shoulders, waist and knees, weight on one leg, arms mid-gesture. The three directions share the drawing and differ in finish. Full body, 40 px bust and five moods; light and dark side by side, people identical in both.

Pure flat colour, no shading at all. The calmest and cheapest to keep consistent.

Flat, plus one soft shadow tone along the side away from the light. Gives the body volume.
